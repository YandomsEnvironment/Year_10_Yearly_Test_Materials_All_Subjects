💡 The Golden "Error Log" Rule

Whenever you select an incorrect answer in an interactive test, don't just click next. Read the green explanation box, write down the one concept you missed in your notebook, and re-attempt that question 2 days later. This simple habit guarantees an A or B in your yearly examinations.
